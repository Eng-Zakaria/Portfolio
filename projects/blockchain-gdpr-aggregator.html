The Problem

Blockchain and market data arrive from many endpoints — Ethereum nodes, Bitcoin RPC, and exchange APIs — while PII can hide in memos, labels, and metadata. Serving that data without masking, retention, and erasure support creates compliance risk.

The aggregator puts a compliance gate in front of analytics: mask and encrypt sensitive fields, record every processing step with timing, and support Article 20 export plus erasure trails before data reaches search, storage, and APIs.

Approach & Implementation

  • Sources: Ethereum via web3.py HTTP provider, Bitcoin via bitcoinlib JSON-RPC, and market APIs — Binance via ccxt, CoinGecko, and Alpha Vantage.
  • Compliance gate: Regex PII masking for email, phone, SSN, cards, IBAN, and wallet addresses with partial, full, hash, and tokenize modes; Fernet encryption; audit-log table with processing_time_ms and Art. 20 export plus erasure trails.
  • Analytics: pandas, numpy, scipy, and sklearn flow analytics over the cleansed datasets.
  • Storage: PostgreSQL 15 plus Elasticsearch 8.11, MongoDB 7, and Redis 7.
  • Scheduling & serving: Celery workers (concurrency 4) with beat, FastAPI on :8000 behind Nginx, batch size 1000, rate limits of 100 requests per minute and 1000 per hour.
  • Observability: Prometheus plus Grafana and Sentry.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: PII in chain and market payloads

Emails, phones, card numbers, IBANs, and wallet-linked labels can appear anywhere in free-text fields across chains and APIs.

Solution: Masking gate with modes

Applied regex masking across email, phone, SSN, cards, IBAN, and wallet patterns with partial, full, hash, and tokenize modes plus Fernet encryption before storage or analytics.

Challenge: Provable retention and erasure

Compliance needs proof of what ran, how long it took, and what was exported or erased.

Solution: Timed audit log

Records each step in an audit-log table with processing_time_ms, supporting Art. 20 export and erasure trails across the 365-day retention and 2555-day audit trail windows.

Outcome

The aggregator funnels Ethereum, Bitcoin, and market feeds through a masking and audit gate into PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, MongoDB, and Redis, served by Celery-scheduled workers and a rate-limited FastAPI behind Nginx.

Rate limits hold at 100 requests per minute and 1000 per hour with batch size 1000, backed by Prometheus, Grafana, and Sentry. Local project — no public GitHub link.
